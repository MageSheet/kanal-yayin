Privacy Policy — Kanal Yayin

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Kanal Yayin is used only by its operator to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and to read those channels' YouTube Analytics.

  • It does not collect, store or share data about any other person.
  • Access tokens for the operator's own channels are stored only on the operator's computer and are never shared with third parties.
  • Google user data is used only to upload, update and measure the operator's own videos, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
